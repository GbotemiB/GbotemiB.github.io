**PyPSA-Earth** is a highly resolved, open-source global energy system model. As a core contributor at the Open Energy Transition, I focus on integrating Concentrated Solar Power (CSP) with thermal storage capabilities into the model.

Key contributions include:
- Developing mathematical formulations for CSP technologies.
- Implementing Monte Carlo simulations for grid expansion under uncertainty.
- Optimizing Python-based workflows using xarray and pandas to handle terabytes of energy data.

The project enables African nations to plan grid expansions accurately, ensuring sustainable and resilient energy futures.
