[PyPSA-Earth](https://pypsa-earth.readthedocs.io/en/latest/) is an open-source energy system model that can be built for any country in the world. As an Energy Systems Modeler at [Open Energy Transition](https://openenergytransition.org/), and a maintainer and documentation lead for the project, I work on:

- **Concentrated solar power with thermal storage**: modeling CSP plants and their storage within the framework.
- **Uncertainty quantification**: a Monte Carlo simulation module that lets users quantify uncertainty in national energy plans.
- **Open grid data**: a comparative geographic analysis of ENTSO-E and OpenStreetMap grid data to move the framework onto fully open data.
- **Sector coupling**: contributions to a fully sector-coupled model of the United States energy system.
- **Community and documentation**: reviewing contributions and leading the overhaul of the documentation to make it easier for new users to start.

I also taught energy system modeling with PyPSA-Earth as an invited lecturer at the International Centre for Hydropower workshop in Nairobi (2024).
