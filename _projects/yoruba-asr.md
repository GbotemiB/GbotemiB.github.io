This project aims to bridge the gap in speech recognition for multilingual African communities. We developed one of the first robust Automatic Speech Recognition (ASR) systems for **Yoruba-English code-switched speech**.

Key highlights:
- Curated a novel 100-hour speech corpus from radio broadcasts and interviews.
- Fine-tuned state-of-the-art multilingual models like Whisper and MMS.
- Achieved state-of-the-art Word Error Rate (WER) on the evaluation set.
- Published at NAACL 2025 and awarded at the Deep Learning Indaba 2025.
