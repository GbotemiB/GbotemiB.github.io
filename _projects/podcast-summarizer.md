A web app for exploring podcast episodes through generated summaries, guest details, highlights and chapters. Users can pick an existing feed or add a new one by RSS link.

- The summarization model was developed in Google Colab.
- The backend runs on Modal Labs.
- The front end is a Streamlit app: [try it live](https://podcastsummarizer-corise.streamlit.app/).

Built with Python, Streamlit and Modal Labs.
