I am an Artificial Intelligence Researcher and Energy Systems Modeler. My research spans **speech and language technology for low-resource African languages** and **open-source macro-energy system modeling**. 

Currently, I am pursuing my Master of Science in Engineering Artificial Intelligence at [Carnegie Mellon University Africa](https://www.africa.engineering.cmu.edu/).

At [Lyngual Labs](https://lynguallabs.org/), I work on bridging the gap in speech recognition for multilingual communities. I co-led the curation of a 100-hour Yoruba-English code-switched speech corpus and focus on fine-tuning large language models and ASR systems for African linguistic diversity.

Additionally, as an Energy Systems Modeler at [Open Energy Transition](https://openenergytransition.org/), I contribute to [PyPSA-Earth](https://pypsa-earth.readthedocs.io/en/latest/), an open-source global energy system model. I specialize in integrating concentrated solar power (CSP) with thermal storage and implementing Monte Carlo simulation modules for uncertainty quantification in national energy plans.

I am passionate about building AI pipelines from data collection to deployment, and I actively teach machine learning and data engineering to aspiring professionals across Africa.
