Hi, I'm Emmanuel. I am an AI researcher and energy systems modeler, currently pursuing an M.S. in Engineering Artificial Intelligence at [Carnegie Mellon University Africa](https://www.africa.engineering.cmu.edu/) in Kigali.

At [Lyngual Labs](https://lynguallabs.org/), I work on speech recognition for African languages, with a focus on Yoruba-English code-switching. I co-led the collection of a 100-hour code-switched speech corpus, and our work on adapting multilingual models to it received the **Best Paper Award at Deep Learning Indaba 2025**.

At [Open Energy Transition](https://openenergytransition.org/), I contribute to [PyPSA-Earth](https://pypsa-earth.readthedocs.io/en/latest/), an open-source global energy system model, where I am also a maintainer and documentation lead. My work there includes modeling concentrated solar power and quantifying uncertainty in national energy plans.

In short, I work on:

- Speech and language technology for low-resource African languages
- AI for education
- Open-source energy system modeling

I also enjoy teaching, and have taught machine learning, data analysis and Python to students and working professionals across Africa.
