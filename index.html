Emmanuel Bolarinwa

M.S. in Engineering AI | CMU Africa

Carnegie Mellon University Africa

Kigali, Rwanda / Lagos, Nigeria

I am an Artificial Intelligence Researcher and Energy Systems Modeler. My research spans speech and language technology for low-resource African languages and open-source macro-energy system modeling.

Currently, I am pursuing my Master of Science in Engineering Artificial Intelligence at Carnegie Mellon University Africa.

At Lyngual Labs, I work on bridging the gap in speech recognition for multilingual communities. I co-led the curation of a 100-hour Yoruba-English code-switched speech corpus and focus on fine-tuning large language models and ASR systems for African linguistic diversity.

Additionally, as an Energy Systems Modeler at Open Energy Transition, I contribute to PyPSA-Earth, an open-source global energy system model. I specialize in integrating concentrated solar power (CSP) with thermal storage and implementing Monte Carlo simulation modules for uncertainty quantification in national energy plans.

I am passionate about building AI pipelines from data collection to deployment, and I actively teach machine learning and data engineering to aspiring professionals across Africa.
